About

Fieldwork, of a different kind.

By day I'm a PhD researcher working in computational genomics. By the days off, I'm usually somewhere unfamiliar with my camera and no fixed itinerary. Photography started as a way to slow down between conference deadlines, and turned into a habit of noticing how cities are built and how landscapes hold light.

Each album here is a place I've spent real time in, walked through more than once, photographed slowly rather than in passing.
